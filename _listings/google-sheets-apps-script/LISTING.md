## 6. FAQ
- **Do I need to install anything?** No. The script lives in your Google Sheet and runs on Google's servers.
- **Is my data safe?** I work on a copy with the access you grant, and you can remove my access as soon as the job is done.
- **What if my request is bigger than a tier?** Message me first; I will quote a custom offer.
- **Will it keep working?** Yes, with no ongoing fee. Google's free quotas (for example, daily email limits) apply and I design within them.
- **Can you fix or extend an existing script?** Yes. Send it over and I will tell you which tier fits.
